How it works

From footpath to feed in three taps.

No login. No forms. Anonymous by default.

  1. 1

    Snap & drop a pin

    Open ManaChetta, take one photo of the garbage pile, illegal dump or broken drain. We capture the geo-tag automatically.

  2. 2

    We auto-detect your ward, MLA & MP

    Your coordinates are mapped to a GHMC ward. We attach the MLA of that Assembly constituency and the Hyderabad MP responsible — so there's no confusion about who's accountable.

  3. 3

    One tap to GHMC & the public map

    A report card with the photo, landmark and representatives is pinned to the public map. Tap Send to GHMC to push it to the GHMC complaints helpline with all the details pre-filled.

About

Built by citizens, for Hyderabad.

ManaChetta is an open, citizen-run mirror of NammaKasa — the Bengaluru project that turned one photo into a public accountability map. We're applying the same idea to Hyderabad's GHMC wards, because our footpaths deserve the same visibility.

No ads. No ownership by any political party. Just a public map and a public leaderboard. If you'd like to contribute ward data, MLA information or help run the platform, get in touch.

We also publish research on the people who actually clear this waste. Read Heat is not gender-neutral — on how heatwaves hit the women in India's informal waste, vending and sanitation work.
