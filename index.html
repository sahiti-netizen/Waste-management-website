How ReLoop works

Recycle in four steps. Get paid for it.

No app install. Free pickup. Points credited the same day.

  1. 1

    Learn what's recyclable

    Search any item in our guide and we'll tell you whether it's recyclable, how to prep it, and how many points it's worth per kilogram.

  2. 2

    Pick a local recycler

    We surface the nearest verified recyclers based on your location and the materials you have — paper, plastic, e-waste, glass, you name it.

  3. 3

    Free doorstep pickup

    Schedule a slot, hand over the bag at your door. Our partner weighs it, pays you in Green Points the same day.

  4. 4

    Spend your Green Points

    Cash out to UPI, redeem vouchers, or shop sustainable goods on our marketplace — all powered by points you've earned.

    Why we built ReLoop

    Recycling shouldn't feel like a chore.

    Indians generate 62 million tonnes of waste a year and only about a quarter is processed. Most of it is recyclable — it just lacks a path to the right person. ReLoop closes that loop: an honest guide, a verified collector, fair pay, and a marketplace where your rewards do something useful.

    We don't collect personal data beyond what's needed for a pickup. Points are stored locally until you sign in. The shop is run by partners we vet for environmental and labour standards.
