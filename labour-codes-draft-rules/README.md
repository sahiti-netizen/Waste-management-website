# Draft Rules under the Labour Codes — Telangana & Gujarat

This folder contains an extraction of the **state draft rules** notified by the
Governments of **Telangana** and **Gujarat** under India's four labour codes:

1. **Code on Wages, 2019** (Central Act No. 29 of 2019)
2. **Industrial Relations Code, 2020** (Central Act No. 35 of 2020)
3. **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020** (Central Act No. 37 of 2020)
4. **Code on Social Security, 2020** (Central Act No. 36 of 2020)

## Background

The four labour codes consolidate 29 central labour laws. The codes came into
force on **21 November 2025**, and the Government of India notified the final
central rules on **8 May 2026** (Code on Wages (Central) Rules 2026, Industrial
Relations (Central) Rules 2026, OSH (Central) Rules 2026 and Social Security
(Central) Rules 2026). Because "labour" is on the Concurrent List, each state
must also frame its own rules for establishments where the state is the
"appropriate Government".

- **Telangana** notified its draft rules in two waves — the Wages and IR draft
  rules on **29 September 2021**, and the OSH and Social Security draft rules on
  **28 January 2022**. State-level rules were still being aligned with the final
  central rules as of mid-2026.
- **Gujarat** was one of only two states (with Arunachal Pradesh) that had
  promulgated **final rules under all four codes** by the time the codes came
  into force on 21 November 2025. Its rules began as drafts/final notifications
  in October–December 2021 and have since been amended (notably the Social
  Security (Gujarat) (Amendment) Rules, 2025 and 2026).

## Status snapshot

| Code | Telangana draft rules | Gujarat rules |
|---|---|---|
| Code on Wages | Code on Wages (Telangana State) Rules, 2021 — draft notified 29 Sep 2021 (G.O.Rt.No. 480) | Code on Wages (Gujarat) Rules, 2021 — notified 5 Oct 2021 |
| Industrial Relations | Industrial Relations (Telangana State) Rules, 2021 — draft notified 29 Sep 2021 | Industrial Relations (Gujarat) Rules, 2021 — notified 5 Oct 2021 |
| OSH & Working Conditions | OSH (Telangana) Rules, 2022 — draft notified 28 Jan 2022 | Gujarat OSH Rules, 2021 — draft notified 30 Dec 2021; re-issued 2025 |
| Social Security | Code on Social Security (Telangana) Rules, 2022 — draft notified 28 Jan 2022 (published 28 Feb 2022) | Code on Social Security (Gujarat) Rules, 2021 — draft notified 16 Nov 2021; amended 2025, 2026 |
