# Code on Wages (Gujarat) Rules, 2021

**Parent statute:** Code on Wages, 2019 (Central Act No. 29 of 2019)
**Notified by:** Labour, Skill Development and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat
**Notification:** Dated **5 October 2021** (draft first published earlier in 2021;
Gujarat was among the first states to finalize rules under all four codes).

## Extent and commencement

- Extends to the **whole of the State of Gujarat**.
- In force from the commencement of the Code on Wages, 2019.

## Key provisions

### Minimum wages (Chapter II)

- Prescribes the **manner of calculating the minimum rate of wages**,
  following the standard working-class family criteria (equivalent of three
  adult consumption units: food intake of 2,700 calories per day per unit,
  clothing, housing rent, fuel/electricity/miscellaneous at prescribed
  percentages, plus children's education, medical needs, festivities and
  contingencies).
- A **monthly wage cycle** is prescribed for minimum-wage compliance.
- The national **floor wage** notified by the Central Government acts as the
  lower bound — the state cannot fix minimum wages below it.

### Partial day's work

- An employee who has worked **not less than four hours** in a working day is
  eligible for minimum wages computed by dividing the daily minimum wage rate
  by eight hours, **with a fifteen per cent rise**, the quotient stepped up to
  the nearest paisa.

### Working day and overtime

- Normal working day framework of 8 hours with rest intervals; overtime at
  twice the ordinary rate per the Code.

### Payment of dues and claims (Chapter V)

- Procedures for payment of dues, **deposit of undisbursed dues**, and filing
  of claims before the appointed authority.

### Contractor liability

- Explicit provisions on the **employer's liability for wages of contract
  labour** where the contractor fails to pay.

### Bonus, registers and returns

- Bonus computation and disbursal procedures; consolidated registers, wage
  slips and electronic maintenance of records; annual returns; and the
  Inspector-cum-Facilitator inspection scheme.
