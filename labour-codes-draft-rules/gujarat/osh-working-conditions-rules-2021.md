# Gujarat Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Rules, 2021 — Draft

**Parent statute:** Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Central Act No. 37 of 2020)
**Rule-making power:** Sections 133 and 135 of the OSH Code, 2020 read with Section 24 of the General Clauses Act, 1897
**Notified by:** Labour, Skill Development and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat
**Notification:** Draft issued **30 December 2021**. A consolidated
"Gujarat Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions Rules, 2025" was
subsequently published in the Gujarat Government Gazette (Extraordinary) ahead
of the codes coming into force on 21 November 2025.

## Extent and applicability

- Extends to the **whole of the State of Gujarat**.
- Applies to all **factories, establishments, and building or other
  construction work** relating to any establishment for which the appropriate
  Government is the State Government under the Code.

## Rules repealed

- Gujarat Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment
  and Condition of Services) Rules, 2003
- Inter-State Migrant Workmen (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of
  Service) (Gujarat) Rules, 1981

## Key provisions

### Registration

- An employer/occupier seeking registration for an establishment/factory not
  already registered applies **electronically in Form 1** on the **portal of
  the Commissioner of Labour**, giving details of the establishment/factory
  and uploading documents: registration proof, and proof of identity and
  address of the employer(s)/occupier as specified in the form.
- Single electronic registration replaces the multiple registrations required
  under the repealed enactments.

### Duties of employers

- Maintain a hazard-free workplace, comply with the Code's health and safety
  standards, provide annual health check-ups for prescribed employee
  categories, and issue appointment letters.

### Welfare and working conditions

- Prescribes welfare facilities (canteens, restrooms, first aid, creches,
  drinking water, washing facilities) by establishment class; hours of work,
  leave with wages, and overtime per the Code.

### Contract labour, migrant workers and construction workers

- Contractor licensing, welfare and record-keeping obligations for contract
  labour and inter-state migrant workers; safety and welfare provisions for
  building and other construction workers folded in from the repealed 2003
  rules.

### Safety administration

- Safety committees and safety officers above prescribed thresholds; notice
  of accidents and dangerous occurrences; powers of the Chief
  Inspector-cum-Facilitator.
