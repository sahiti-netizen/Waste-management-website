# Code on Social Security (Gujarat) Rules, 2021 — Draft (with 2025/2026 amendments)

**Parent statute:** Code on Social Security, 2020 (Central Act No. 36 of 2020)
**Notified by:** Labour and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat
**Notification:** Draft issued **16 November 2021**, to be taken into
consideration after **45 days** from publication. Final rules were in place by
the time the codes commenced (21 November 2025), and were amended by the
**Code on Social Security (Gujarat) (Amendment) Rules, 2025** and the
**Code on Social Security (Gujarat) (Amendment) Rules, 2026**.

## Extent

- Extends to the **State of Gujarat**.

## Rules superseded

- Gujarat Maternity Benefit Rules, 1964
- Payment of Gratuity (Gujarat) Rules, 1973
- Gujarat Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1967

## Key provisions

### Gratuity (Chapter II)

- Procedures for nomination, determination and payment of gratuity; specifies
  the **banks/financial institutions in which gratuity amounts for minors
  shall be invested** (Section 53(1) of the Code).
- Under the amendment rules: **fixed-term employees qualify for gratuity after
  one year** of service (down from five); the competent authority must notify
  an applicant of the **status of their application within 15 days**; where a
  claim is admissible, gratuity is payable **within 30 days**.

### Maternity benefit (Chapter III)

- Administration of maternity benefit claims, notice formats, proof
  requirements, and payment procedures (superseding the 1964 state rules).

### Employee compensation

- Claims procedure, deposit and distribution of compensation (superseding the
  Gujarat Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1967).

### Building and other construction workers — cess

- The **Building Workers Welfare Board** is constituted for collection of cess
  and welfare of building workers.
- **Cess under Section 100(1)** must be paid **electronically, in advance or
  before work starts**.
- **House owners building for their own use above ₹50 lakh** must pay cess at
  **plan approval**; **builders/developers** pay cess electronically **when
  submitting plans for approval**.
- The Board may pay **group insurance premiums**, frame **educational
  initiatives for beneficiaries' children**, and cover **medical treatment for
  major ailments** (powers added by the amendment rules).

### Unorganised, gig and platform workers

- Registration of unorganised workers and channels for welfare schemes for
  gig/platform workers under the Code's framework.
