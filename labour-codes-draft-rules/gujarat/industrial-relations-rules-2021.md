# Industrial Relations (Gujarat) Rules, 2021

**Parent statute:** Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Central Act No. 35 of 2020)
**Notified by:** Labour, Skill Development and Employment Department, Government of Gujarat
**Notification:** Dated **5 October 2021**.

## Extent and commencement

- Extends to the **whole of Gujarat** in respect of industrial establishments
  for which the State Government is the appropriate Government.
- Comes into force from the commencement of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Key provisions

### Works committee

- A **works committee** is to be constituted with an **equal number of
  representatives from the employer and workers' sides**, with membership
  capped at **20**.

### Grievance redressal committee

- A **Grievance Redressal Committee** with equal employer–worker
  representation, membership capped at **10**.

### Standing orders

- Regulates **drafting, certification and modification** of standing orders.
- An employer who **adopts the Central Government's model standing orders**
  must **electronically intimate the certifying officer** of the specific date
  from which the model standing orders are adopted.

### Trade unions and negotiation

- Procedures for trade union registration, recognition of the negotiating
  union / negotiating council, and related returns.

### Dispute resolution machinery

- Procedural framework for **conciliation, adjudication (Industrial
  Tribunals), and other mechanisms** for resolving industrial disputes under
  the Code, including forms for strike/lock-out notices, lay-off,
  retrenchment and closure, and the worker **re-skilling fund**.
