# Code on Wages (Telangana State) Rules, 2021 — Draft

**Parent statute:** Code on Wages, 2019 (Central Act No. 29 of 2019)
**Notified by:** Labour, Employment, Training & Factories Department, Government of Telangana
**Notification:** G.O.Rt.No. 480, dated 29 September 2021 (draft published in the gazette for objections/suggestions)

## Extent and commencement

- Extends to the **whole of the State of Telangana**.
- Comes into force on the date of commencement of the Code on Wages, 2019.

## Rules repealed on commencement

The draft rules repeal all rules made by the State Government under:

- Payment of Wages Act, 1936
- Minimum Wages Act, 1948
- Payment of Bonus Act, 1965
- Equal Remuneration Act, 1976

## Key provisions

### Working hours and rest

- A normal working day of **8 hours a day / 48 hours a week**.
- A **weekly rest day**, ordinarily Sunday, though the employer may fix any
  other day of the week as the rest day for an employee or class of employees.
- **Overtime** limited to 6 hours per week, payable at **double** the normal
  hourly wage rate.

### Minimum wages

- Prescribes the manner of computing the minimum rate of wages (following the
  central template: standard working-class family criteria — food, clothing,
  housing, fuel/electricity, children's education, medical, festivities and
  contingencies).
- Continues the display obligations (notices of minimum wage rates at the
  workplace) previously required under the Telangana Minimum Wages Rules, 1960.

### Bonus

- Bonus eligibility on completing **30 days of work** in an accounting year,
  with procedures for computation and disbursal under the Code.

### Registers, records and wage slips

- **Form I** — Register of Wages, Overtime, Fines, and Deductions for damage
  and loss.
- **Form IV** — Employee Register.
- **Form V** — Wage Slip.
- Records may be maintained electronically.

### Claims and inspections

- Procedures for filing claims (unpaid/short-paid wages, bonus) before the
  designated authorities, and an inspection scheme built around the
  **Inspector-cum-Facilitator** model of the Code.
