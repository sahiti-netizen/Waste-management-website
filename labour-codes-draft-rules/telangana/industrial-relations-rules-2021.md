# Industrial Relations (Telangana State) Rules, 2021 — Draft

**Parent statute:** Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Central Act No. 35 of 2020)
**Rule-making power:** Section 99 of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020
**Notified by:** Labour, Employment, Training & Factories Department, Government of Telangana
**Notification:** Draft published in the gazette on 29 September 2021; to be
taken into consideration after **30 days** from the date the gazette copies
were made available to the public.

## Extent and commencement

- Extends to the **whole of the State of Telangana** in respect of industrial
  establishments and matters for which the State Government is the
  "appropriate Government".
- Comes into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette.
- Supersedes the state rules under the earlier industrial relations
  legislation (Industrial Disputes Act, Industrial Employment (Standing
  Orders) Act, Trade Unions Act as applicable to the state).

## Key provisions

### Standing orders

- Standing orders are **mandatory for industrial establishments employing 300
  or more workers**.
- Employers must prepare **draft standing orders within six months** of the
  commencement of the Code, based on the model standing orders.
- Standing orders govern conditions of employment: classification of workers,
  work hours, leave, shift working, attendance, termination procedures,
  suspension and disciplinary action.
- Procedure prescribed for certification, modification, and appeals before the
  certifying officer.

### Works committee and grievance redressal

- Constitution of **works committees** in industrial establishments (equal
  representation of employer and workers) for establishments of the prescribed
  size.
- **Grievance Redressal Committees** with joint employer–worker representation
  for resolving individual grievances.

### Trade unions and negotiating union/council

- Procedures for registration of trade unions, and for recognition of the
  **sole negotiating union** or constitution of a **negotiating council**
  under the Code's criteria (51% membership threshold for a sole negotiating
  union; 20% per-seat threshold for a negotiating council).

### Dispute resolution

- Procedural framework for **conciliation** proceedings, reference and
  adjudication before **Industrial Tribunals**, arbitration, and notices of
  strikes and lock-outs (14-day prior notice regime under the Code).
- Forms and procedures for retrenchment, lay-off and closure applications
  (thresholds per the Code — prior permission needed at 300+ workers).
- **Worker re-skilling fund** contribution mechanics (15 days' wages per
  retrenched worker).
