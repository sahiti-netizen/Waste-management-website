# Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (Telangana) Rules, 2022 — Draft

**Parent statute:** Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Central Act No. 37 of 2020)
**Notified by:** Labour, Employment, Training & Factories Department, Government of Telangana
**Notification:** Draft dated **28 January 2022**, with a **45-day** window for objections and suggestions.

## Extent and applicability

- Extends to and is in force in the **whole of the State of Telangana**.
- Applies to all establishments for which the **State Government is the
  appropriate Government** under the Code (factories, contract-labour
  establishments, building & other construction work, motor transport,
  plantations, shops/establishments per the Code's thresholds).

## Key provisions

### Employer duties

- Maintain a workplace **free from hazards** likely to cause injury or
  occupational disease.
- Comply with all health, safety and working-condition standards prescribed
  under the Code and rules.
- Provide **free annual health examinations** for prescribed categories of
  employees.
- Issue appointment letters to every employee in the prescribed format.

### Registration and notice of commencement

- Factories, mines, contract-labour establishments and construction projects
  must **notify the government before commencing** any industry, business or
  occupation; the notices are acknowledged **electronically**.
- Single registration regime for establishments per the Code (replacing
  multiple registrations under the repealed acts).

### Working conditions and welfare

- Standards for health, sanitation, drinking water, ventilation, canteens,
  restrooms, first aid and creche facilities per establishment category.
- Working hours, leave with wages, and overtime per the Code's framework;
  provisions for women workers' employment (including with consent and safety
  safeguards for work between 7 pm and 6 am, per the Code).

### Safety committees and officers

- Constitution of **safety committees** and appointment of **safety officers**
  in establishments above the prescribed thresholds.

### Contract labour and migrant workers

- Licensing of contractors, welfare obligations toward contract labour and
  inter-state migrant workers, and journey allowance/records requirements.
