# Code on Social Security (Telangana) Rules, 2022 — Draft

**Parent statute:** Code on Social Security, 2020 (Central Act No. 36 of 2020)
**Notified by:** Labour, Employment, Training & Factories Department, Government of Telangana
**Notification:** Draft dated **28 January 2022**; preliminary notification
published **28 February 2022**.

## Extent

- Extends to the **whole of the State of Telangana** for matters where the
  State Government is the appropriate Government under the Code.

## Key provisions

### Registration and contributions (EPF / ESI)

- Registration of establishments required to pay **EPF** and **ESI** benefits
  to workers, with detailed procedures for payment of benefits.
- **ESI contribution rates** specified: **3.25%** of employee wages as the
  employer's contribution and **0.75%** as the employee's contribution.
- New employees must be registered with their **Aadhaar number** for insurance
  under ESIC.

### Gratuity

- Procedures for determination and payment of **gratuity**, nomination, and
  recovery; competent authority designated for gratuity disputes.

### Employee compensation

- Procedures for claims relating to **employee compensation** (successor to
  the Workmen's Compensation regime), including deposit and distribution of
  compensation and provisions addressing **misuse of benefits**.

### Maternity benefit and creche

- Maternity benefit administration under the Code.
- Every establishment with **50 or more women employees** must provide a
  **creche** facility.

### Building and other construction workers

- Registration of building workers as beneficiaries, collection of **cess**,
  and administration of the state welfare board's schemes.

### Unorganised, gig and platform workers

- Framework for registration of unorganised-sector workers (linked to
  e-Shram), and channels for extending welfare schemes to gig and platform
  workers per the Code.
  (Telangana separately pursued a dedicated Gig and Platform Workers
  (Registration, Social Security and Welfare) Bill in 2025.)
