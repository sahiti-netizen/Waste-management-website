Important — how this handles Aadhaar & biometrics. This prototype does not capture, store or match biometric data, and it is not connected to UIDAI. Aadhaar-based biometric authentication is restricted to UIDAI-authorised agencies under the Aadhaar Act — a private employer cannot run its own Aadhaar biometric matching. Aadhaar is stored here only as a masked reference number (last 4 digits) with the employee's consent. All data stays in your browser (localStorage); nothing is uploaded.
